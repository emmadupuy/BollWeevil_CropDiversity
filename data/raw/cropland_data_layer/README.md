# Cropland Data Layer

**Source:** USDA National Agricultural Statistics Service, [Cropland Data Layer](https://www.nass.usda.gov/Research_and_Science/Cropland/Release/index.php). Obtain the original classified national rasters needed by the construction scripts. To reproduce Supplementary Figure 1, place the 2021 national 30 m raster at `2021_30m_cdls/2021_30m_cdls.tif`. The CDL is also used to train and validate the agricultural-land model underlying the machine-learning county crosswalk. The large rasters are not redistributed here.
