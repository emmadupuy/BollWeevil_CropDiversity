# LANDFIRE topography

**Source:** U.S. Department of the Interior and U.S. Department of Agriculture, [LANDFIRE data](https://landfire.gov/data/lf2020). The historical workflow uses the LF 2020 elevation and slope-degree products identified locally as `LF2020_Elev_220_CONUS` and `LF2020_SlpD_220_CONUS`. These rasters are used to construct county ruggedness and the topographic predictors for the agricultural-land model. Retain the provider metadata and confirm the exact release when downloading. The rasters are not redistributed here.
