# County boundaries and crosswalk geography

**Sources:** [U.S. Census Bureau cartographic boundary files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/cartographic-boundary.html), [U.S. Census Bureau TIGER/Line files](https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/tiger-line-file.html), [Newberry Library Atlas of Historical County Boundaries](https://digital.newberry.org/ahcb/), and [IPUMS NHGIS geographic crosswalks and GIS files](https://www.nhgis.org/geographic-crosswalks). Place the extracted 2016 county cartographic boundary components under `cb_2016_us_county_500k/`; they generate the county coordinates used by the regressions. Supplementary Figure 1 uses the 2021 TIGER/Line county shapefile under `tl_2021_us_county/`, while the historical crosswalk construction uses the Newberry and NHGIS geometries. Preserve all shapefile components. These source files are not redistributed here.
